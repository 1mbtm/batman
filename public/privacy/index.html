Privacy Notice

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This privacy notice explains how batman-7y3.pages.dev — the personal and professional portfolio of Muhammed Arqam Khan (Arqam) — handles first-party visitor analytics, optional GPS location consent, referral attribution, and visitor data rights.

1. First-Party Visitor Analytics

This portfolio uses a lightweight, self-hosted analytics system powered by Cloudflare Pages Functions and Cloudflare D1. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell visitor data. The following non-identifying telemetry is collected:

  • Anonymous Visitor & Session IDs — Randomly generated first-party UUIDs stored in your browser to estimate unique visitors, sessions, and first/last visit timestamps.
  • Page Views & Referral Attribution — Visited page paths, referrer domain/URL, and optional campaign parameters (such as ?ref=campaign_name) for first-touch and last-touch attribution. We never attempt to identify visitors' social media usernames.
  • Device & Browser Metadata — Form factor (Mobile, Desktop, or Tablet), operating system, browser name and version, and screen/viewport dimensions.

2. Location Tracking: Optional GPS Consent vs. IP Estimates

  • No GPS Collection Without Explicit Permission — Browser GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, GPS accuracy in meters, and timestamp) are collected only if you explicitly click to opt in and grant browser geolocation permission. Your consent timestamp is recorded in our audit log.
  • Approximate IP-Based Geolocation Fallback — When GPS permission is not granted, the server reads Cloudflare edge network headers to estimate approximate country, state/region, city, and postal code. IP-based location is an approximate network estimate only and is never treated or presented as an exact physical location.
  • No Raw IP Storage — Raw IP addresses are processed server-side and are never permanently stored in our database. Only a daily-rotated, salted one-way hash is used transiently for security and deduplication.

3. Consent Withdrawal & Data Deletion Rights

You can withdraw your GPS consent or delete all analytics records associated with your browser at any time:

  • Open Location & Data Settings in the portfolio footer on the homepage and click Withdraw GPS Consent (which immediately clears your stored GPS coordinates and reverts to approximate IP-only mode) or Delete My Analytics Data (which permanently deletes your visitor record, sessions, page visits, and location consent history from our database).
  • Alternatively, you may email 1m.btmnnn@gmail.com to request data deletion.

4. Data Retention Period

Analytics visit and session records are retained for a configurable period of 90 days by default (ANALYTICS_RETENTION_DAYS=90), after which expired records are automatically purged.

5. Contact

For privacy-related questions or deletion requests, contact Arqam at 1m.btmnnn@gmail.com.
